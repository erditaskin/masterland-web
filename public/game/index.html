Şehirler

How to play Masterland

Collect items from producers, merge matching pairs and fill orders from the order board. Build your specialties as new production chains unlock.

World Atlas

Produce, merge, deliver

Board

Grow wheat in the Wheat Field. Merge two matching items on the 6 × 9 board and fill orders.

Cities

Start with fields and mills in Meadowbrook. Discover cookie, tart and chocolate chains in Baker’s Market.

Collection

Track your discoveries by production chain. Complete specialty orders to earn city titles and rewards.

Specialize in every city

World Atlas

Complete specialty orders to progress through seven title ranks. Reach the same rank in every specialty within a city to unlock a combined city title and reward.
