Temel tahta, birleştirme ve siparişler çevrimdışı çalışır. Bulut kaydı ve ödüllü reklamlar bağlantı gerektirir.

Player questions

Get support
Can I play offline?

Core board play, merging and orders work offline. Cloud saves and rewarded ads require a connection.

Do I need an account?

You can start as a guest. Sign in with a supported provider to sync your progress to the cloud.

How do I earn mastery titles?

Complete orders for a specialty. Reach the same rank in all specialties to earn the combined city title.
